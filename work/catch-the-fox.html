Catch the Fox

Catch the Fox won Emergenza Norway in 2023 and went on to represent Norway at the international final in Germany. With Northern Norwegian roots and a sound that moves between rock and alternative music, the band asked us to carry its raw energy into a visual expression. We created artwork for an EP, two singles and a long-sleeve shirt, centred on two foxes locked in a yin-yang chase.
