Wedding Design

A wedding is one of life’s most significant days, and its visual expression should feel just as personal. We work with couples to bring their ideas and stories into hand-drawn invitations, posters, illustrations, menus and place cards. The work shown here draws on meaningful places, personal symbols and favourite colours to create a distinct character for each celebration.
