Why cellulose

While working as a designer at Wray Ward, Ida developed the visual direction, storyboard, and animation for Greenfiber’s “Why Cellulose” campaign video. The piece explains the benefits of cellulose insulation through a mix of illustration, motion, and educational storytelling — from recycled paper and thermal performance to sound absorption, fire resistance, and sustainability. Created in collaboration with the wider Wray Ward team, the project is featured in the Elida portfolio as an example of Ida’s experience in visual storytelling, design, and animation prior to founding Elida.
