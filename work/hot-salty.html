Hot & Salty

We created Hot & Salty’s identity for Ramsalt, home to Bodø’s largest hotel, restaurants and Arctic experiences. Hot & Salty is a floating sauna village where guests move between sauna heat, a heated saltwater pool and the cold sea. We built the communication strategy around contrasts: hot and cold, inside and outside, light and dark seasons. This idea ran through the logo, visual identity and campaigns, including posters, screen displays, window graphics and printed materials. Each ad gives people a sense of the experience before they arrive.
