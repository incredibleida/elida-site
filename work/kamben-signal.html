Kamben Signal

Kamben is a mountain in western Norway, where signposts guide hikers to the top. This idea of finding direction became the inspiration behind Kamben Signal’s brand identity. An Oslo-based performance analytics consultancy, Kamben Signal helps businesses make sense of the numbers that drive their sales. Combining technical expertise with human connection, they help clients navigate their own terrain. Technical expertise, without the technical distance.

Our design challenge was to bring technical precision and a human feel together in one cohesive identity. Kamben’s topographical lines are integrated into the logo, while analogue photography and rustic shapes bring warmth and character to the visual language. The result is a brand that reflects Kamben Signal’s core philosophy: Performance analytics for humans, by humans.
