What can we help you with?

01

Brand positioning & visual research

Before deciding what you should look like, it's important to understand who you are. Where do you stand in relation to others? What should people recognise you for?

We use research and visual exploration to answer those questions and establish a direction before the design work begins.

Questionnaire & interviews Socratic dialogue Positioning Competitor mapping Moodboards Visual research Visual direction Brand essence
02

Strategic communication

Clear communication starts with a clear voice. We can help you find yours.

What is your message? Who is your audience? What should people feel when finding your brand?

Together with you, we turn these questions into a practical direction for your brand, so that your message, design and communication work together.

Communication strategy Messaging & key messages Tone of voice Audience mapping Brand story Copywriting
03

Design & brand identity

How you look affects how you are understood.

We design how you look, so it fits who you are. We bring logo, typography, colour, imagery and layout into one unified identity you can grow with.

The aim is to give you a recognisable expression that you can handle across different formats.

Logo Typography Colour palette Image style Brand guidelines Templates Merch Presentation design
04

Campaign & concept

Planning a campaign? The strongest campaigns are usually built around one clear idea. When the concept is right, it becomes easier to make decisions about message, design, channels and content.

We help develop that idea and turn it into a campaign that works across formats, from the first headline to the final rollout.

Campaign concept Key visual Headlines & copy Ads & banners Event material Conference presence
05

Social media

Social media is often where people meet you first. It shapes how your your brand is understood.

We help you decide what to talk about, how it should sound and look, and how to build a presence you can keep up over time.

SoMe strategy Content plan Post-templates for your team Production-ready posts LinkedIn Facebook Instagram Carousels Short videos
06

Web design

A website is the place where everything comes together. It should be easy to navigate, clear in what it says, and recognisably yours.

We design the structure, support with content and lead the visual expression, then bring those parts together into a site that works as one whole to represent your brand.

Landing page Website design UX & structure Copy for web Motion on web Launch & handover
07

Animation & motion

Movement helps an idea land, gives information rhythm and invites people to engage with your message.

We create motion with a clear purpose, from animated identities and explainers to data, social content and title sequences.

Animated logo Motion identity Animated data & graphs Explainers
08

Photography

Photography that looks like you.

We plan and produce portraits, team photos, product, workplaces, events and social content, with retouching included.

Portraits & team photos Environment & workplace Event Product Content for social media Image editing & retouching

Illustration

Need something that does not already exist?
We can draw it.

We create original illustrations, characters, sketches and visual elements with a strong artistic point of view. The work is tailored to your style and can be playful or restrained, depending on what the project needs.

Editorial illustration Hand-drawn characters Sketches Maps & diagrams Patterns Custom artwork Icons

Print & production

Design that arrives in your hands, ready to use.

We produce posters, invitations, reports, roll-ups, signage and full campaign sets, from first sketch to final print.

Posters Brochures Reports Roll-ups & signage Invitations Campaign material Print preparation Production

Hello! We’re Elias and Ida, a Norwegian-Swedish duo and the founders of the studio.

The idea behind Elida began in Nepal. Elias was coordinating a renewable energy and food security project with the UN. Ida was designing for American brands from her laptop in Kathmandu. Working in different sectors, we found ourselves asking the same questions: What are we trying to accomplish? Why does it matter? And how can we make it better?

Elias brings a background in philosophy and international development, with experience of turning complicated projects into coordinated work. Ida is a talented artist bringing more than seven years of art direction and graphic design experience, shaping brands across the US and Scandinavia.

We love to challenge ourselves and work with people who dare to think big. Our vision is to make the world more beautiful, and help people and brands become who they are.
